The Future of Storytelling

Plutopia is an AI-native entertainment studio creating original films, series, characters, and digital worlds for the next generation of storytelling. Combining cinematic craft with emerging technology, the studio develops scalable IP through animation, AI-assisted filmmaking, virtual production, music, and social-first storytelling.

At the core of Plutopia is a global social ecosystem with more than 9 million followers and over 5 billion views across platforms. These channels act as a live testing ground where stories, characters, pilots, and products are launched directly to audiences in real time — keeping Plutopia closely connected to internet culture, evolving algorithms, and the cultural zeitgeist.

Plutopia has collaborated with brands including ASUS, Mountain Dew, Microsoft, and Clarks, creating entertainment-driven campaigns that blend storytelling, culture, and technology. The studio provides creative development, AI-assisted production, world-building, branded content, and original IP incubation for both original projects and brand partners.

Founded by award-winning creator Sage Shepard, Plutopia is built on the belief that the future of entertainment lies at the intersection of human creativity and AI. The mission is to pioneer a new model for storytelling — one where creators can move faster, build deeper audience connections, and bring ambitious new worlds to life at scale.
