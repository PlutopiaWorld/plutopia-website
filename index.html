The Future of Storytelling

Plutopia is an AI-powered studio and production company creating cinematic content at the intersection of filmmaking, internet culture, artificial intelligence, and social-first entertainment — developing branded entertainment, music videos, scripted concepts, original series, and story worlds designed to move across screens, platforms, campaigns, products, and owned channels.

Founded by award-winning artist, technologist, and creator Sage Shepard — Plutopia combines cinematic craft, AI filmmaking, animation, music, and social-first storytelling to produce entertainment-driven campaigns and original content for brands, platforms, and audiences.

AI does not need to replace artists or storytellers. It expands what they can make. Human taste, emotion, and world-building stay at the center, while new tools help bring ambitious stories to life faster, bigger, and more imaginatively.
